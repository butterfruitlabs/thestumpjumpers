A River Runs Through It: River Run Playground

Riverside Park between W. 81st & 83rd Sts., Manhattan
Most Recently Reviewed On Sunday, July 19, 2026 | Filed under: Hidden Gems & Worth the Trip & Best Hudson River Greenway Stops
The legendary sandbox, sculptures and all.

This playground is aging, but it's great. It's a true hidden gem: you can't see it from any road, so you have to wander through Riverside Park to find it. That's a treat in itself, because the landscaping around here is first-rate, and there's a very cool giant boulder along the way.

One of the so-bad-they're-great sandbox faces.

What makes River Run unique is its centerpiece: a replica of the Hudson River running right through the middle. “Replica” is being generous — it's really a squiggly concrete stream — but it's a lot of fun. It starts at a splash-pad fountain and winds its way down to a drain near the sandbox. Who doesn't want to float an impromptu boat all the way down it?

The “Hudson River,” winding through the playground.

The other highlight is the legendary sandbox, with its so-bad-they're-great faces molded into the sides by a local artist. I know a few parents who are genuinely creeped out by them and avoid the sandbox completely. I think they're great. There's also a large public restroom right within the park, which is worth its weight in gold.

The weird blue modern climber.

A few plastic pieces of equipment and one weird blue modern climber don't really jive with the rest of the place, but I suppose they add variety, since a stream isn't everyone's idea of fun (and it gets turned off in the winter). Good news for the future: after a community outcry, the city's planned renovation will keep both the river and the sandbox sculptures.

The playground's dedication plaque.

Verdict: worth traveling to. Because it's a bit older, it often gets overlooked, which is exactly why it deserves a visit.

The Stump Report

Overall Fun:Floating boats down a river never gets old.
Originality:A concrete Hudson River and a sandbox full of faces.
Cleanliness:Showing its age, but well loved.
Size:Approx. 25,000 sq ft, our estimate. Plenty of room between the river and the sandbox.
Dad Test:Who doesn't want to float a boat down a river?
Bathrooms:A large public restroom right in the park.

By bike: the Hudson River Greenway runs through Riverside Park just below; head up into the park around 83rd Street. On the run: Riverside Park's winding paths make the approach half the fun; watch for the giant boulder along the way. By train: 1 to 79 St or 86 St, then walk west into Riverside Park.
