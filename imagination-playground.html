Imagination Required: Imagination Playground at Burling Slip

John St. & South St., near the South Street Seaport, Manhattan
Most Recently Reviewed On Sunday, August 2, 2026 | Filed under: Best Avoided
The pipes and horns, doing nothing in particular.

I'd read all about this one before we went. Designed by the architect David Rockwell, Imagination Playground opened in 2010 with a big idea: instead of fixed equipment, give kids loose parts — giant foam blocks, crates, wagons — and let them build their own play. We showed up on a beautiful Sunday ready to build.

Ship-shaped decks and masts — but no blocks.

There were no blocks. No foam, no crates, no loose parts of any kind, nothing like the descriptions I'd read. The interesting-looking tubes and pipes scattered around turned out not to do anything at all. Maybe it's called Imagination Playground because there's nothing to do but use your imagination. We did our best.

A beautiful Sunday, and nobody else here.

The design isn't without charm. The whole playground is loosely boat-shaped and nautical, which suits the setting nicely, with the Seaport's old ships and the ferries right next door. There's a splash pad, though a pretty minimal one. And there's a bathroom right inside the playground, which deserves real credit, even if it was only moderately scuzzy.

The splash pad, such as it was.

The place was deserted the entire time we were there. As we were leaving, another family arrived — they'd taken the ferry over from Brooklyn specially — and they were every bit as disappointed as we were to find it empty and seemingly abandoned. I felt for them.

The in-playground bathroom (moderately scuzzy).

Verdict: best avoided, at least until someone brings the blocks back. If you're downtown, the Battery Playscape is a short walk away and a much better use of a Sunday.

The Stump Report

Overall Fun:No blocks, and the pipes don't do anything.
Originality:The ship theme suits the Seaport. That's the highlight.
Cleanliness:Not filthy, just forgotten.
Size:Approx. 18,000 sq ft, our estimate. A decent footprint with very little to fill it.
Dad Test:Nothing to do for kids, let alone grown-ups.
Bathrooms:Right inside the playground; only moderately scuzzy.

By bike: the East River Greenway runs right outside; from the Hudson River Greenway, cut around the Battery. On the run: the East River Esplanade makes a scenic approach past the Seaport's old ships. By train: 2, 3, 4, 5, A, C, J, or Z to Fulton St, then walk east toward the river. NYC Ferry and the Brooklyn ferries land at Pier 11/Wall St, a few blocks south.
